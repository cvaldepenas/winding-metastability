### 2. Q3 — exact non-recovery is sound, but the channel claim needs a metric

**Direct answer.** The condition

```text
there is no measurable phi with phi(Y) = ell almost surely
```

is exactly the right condition for saying that a single observation of
`Y` does not determine `ell` with zero error under a specified probability
law. It is not, by itself, enough to say that `Y` carries little information
about `ell`, that approximate recovery is hard, or that a controller seeing
the time history of `Y` cannot recover `ell`.

**What is explicit.** R6 combines two different statements: the controller's
potential, added energy, and gain are `sigma(Y)`-measurable, while the label
is not `sigma(Y)`-measurable (packet lines 134-141). Theorem A.3 then proves
the latter for `Y=J`, under any reflection-invariant probability with a
strictly positive density (lines 281-316). The coupled/composed extension is
only sketched in this packet (lines 318-329). The packet also correctly
disclaims semantic content and calls "information honesty" a row name
(lines 389-393). Within that disclosed, exact sense, the theorem is sound.

**What follows only by inference.** For fixed `N`, winding takes finitely
many values. On the standard Borel spaces here, exact non-measurability
implies positive Bayes classification error and positive conditional
entropy, but provides no numerical or parameter-uniform lower bound. The
reflection proof gives a little more: invariance of `(J,w)` under
`(J,w) -> (J,-w)` implies

```text
inf_f P[f(J) != w] >= (1/2) P[w != 0] > 0,
```

where the strict positivity uses the positive mass of `Sigma_1` established
at packet lines 313-316. This bound is an inference, not a stated packet
result, and the packet supplies no lower bound on `P[w != 0]` uniform in
`beta`, budget, coupling, or family.

**Why mutual information needs care.** Requiring `I(ell;Y)=0` would assert
independence and is much stronger than what R6 proves. It is not justified
by reflection: reflection hides the sign of nonzero winding, while `J` may
still reveal information about its magnitude or sector because sector
ground energies differ (lines 57-72). A faithful quantitative strengthening
would instead type one of

```text
inf_f P[f(Y) != ell] >= epsilon,
H(ell | Y) >= h0,
I(ell;Y) <= H(ell) - h0,
```

with the probability law, parameter range, and uniformity of `epsilon` or
`h0` stated. If no quantitative row is wanted, rename the present property
"no exact static decoder" rather than letting "cannot recover" suggest
secrecy or poor statistical predictability.

**Hidden assumptions that must be typed.** R6's summary does not name the
measure governing "a.e."; Theorem A.3 does. It also does not say whether the
channel is one snapshot `sigma(Y)`, the observation filtration
`sigma(Y_s: s <= t)`, or `Y` plus side information. The proof covers only
the first. These distinctions are material for a feedback-channel claim.

### 3. Q5 — the skeleton is coherent but missing several load-bearing types

**Direct answer.** R1-R6 are not wholesale redundant: they separate state
geometry, non-vacuity, persistence, contrast, intervention, and information
restriction. RE is a lifting/composition assertion. The minimum useful
repair is to add or expose the following types.

1. **Process/admissibility row.** State the exact SDE/generator, invariant
   law, reversibility class, control regularity, and whether parameters may
   depend on `beta`. The passive generator is given at lines 75-83, while
   skew/state-dependent families and a reversible-only composition are
   mentioned at lines 89-96; the skeleton itself does not type that split.

2. **Stopping-event row, or an event parameter in R3.** "Persistence" must
   name whether the event is domain exit or sector/label change. The packet
   later proves these are exponentially different objects: domain cap
   versus one-bond saddle (lines 193-247 and 266-273), and repeats the
   distinction in its limits block (lines 396-412). R3's one-line summary
   at lines 121-122 does not expose it.

3. **Quantifier/uniformity row.** Type `q` (fixed point versus compact set or
   `beta`-dependent start), the order of the `beta`, `epsilon`, budget, and
   `delta` quantifiers, and whether bounds are pointwise or uniform over
   class members. Symbolic small-coupling thresholds are admitted at lines
   157-166 and 394-396, but their relationship to the low-temperature limit
   is not stated in the skeleton.

4. **Sharpness-status row.** R3 is a lower-bound certificate. The later SH
   and label-tower results distinguish certified lower rates from true
   two-sided rates and open upper-bound rows (lines 193-247). A skeleton for
   the completed package should type `lower bound / true exponent / open`
   rather than leaving this only in prose caveats.

5. **Control-observation and budget row.** R5 gives gain/no-harm and R6 gives
   static measurability, but the skeleton should say what the budget measures,
   what observations are admissible over time, and what controller cost or
   regularity is imposed. Splitting R6 into (a) controller admissibility and
   (b) label opacity would make both obligations independently testable.

6. **Lifting metadata for RE.** RE is not the same logical kind as R1-R6; it
   says the rows survive embedding into a coupled factor (lines 142-144).
   It should be presented as a closure/lifting theorem with explicit member,
   threshold, and preserved-row quantifiers, rather than as an undifferentiated
   seventh row.

**Limited redundancy/overlap.** If R2 and R6 use the same law, R6 already
implies the first R2 clause: if the label were almost surely constant, a
constant measurable decoder would recover it. R2's positive-mass assertion
for every declared domain is not implied and should remain. R4 and R5(iii)
are not logically duplicates: R4 supplies the base contrast certificate,
while R5(iii) asserts its preservation under intervention. They could be
combined into one parameterized contrast row, but neither can simply be
deleted.

**Answerability limit.** These are design findings against the informal
summary. A formal redundancy proof or a complete replacement skeleton is
not possible because packet lines 111-114 say the exact row texts live in
the M4 artifact, which was outside the allowed input set, and lines 398-400
say the row set is a design choice rather than canonical or exhaustive.

### 4. Q7 — no soundness endorsement is possible; the follow-up demand is substantive

**Direct answer.** I cannot verify that the reversible-action lower bound is
sound or that its `h`-reshaped and coupled applications are legitimate. The
packet gives the desired conclusion, not the theorem or the application.
It proves B.1-B.2 (lines 342-351), defers B.3 (lines 353-361), and expressly
says the geometry-to-rate step is not derived there (lines 363-379). This is
an input insufficiency, not a negative mathematical verdict.

The following must be served in the scoped follow-up before Q7 can close.

1. **Verbatim action theorem.** Supply the exact Freidlin-Wentzell-type
   statement, proof or precise primary theorem pin, action functional, and
   normalization for the generator
   `beta^(-1) Delta - grad(V).grad`. The factor-of-two convention must yield
   the claimed exponent `barrier - V(q)`, not an unstated multiple.

2. **Exit-theorem hypotheses.** Show the theorem covers a compact torus,
   the actual open domain and boundary regularity, fixed starting points
   and any claimed compact uniformity, and the non-isolated rotation-circle
   minimum `C_k` in B.3 (lines 353-356). A theorem assuming an isolated
   hyperbolic equilibrium would not match this packet.

3. **Complete geometry input.** Supply B.3, including the critical-level
   exclusion at `2 kappa` and its separate `N=4` case (lines 357-361), and
   prove that the chosen component has exactly the boundary/cap used by the
   exit theorem.

4. **One hypothesis-matching sheet per potential.** For `J`, `h(J)`,
   `E_epsilon`, and `E_c`, identify the process, domain, cap, ground set,
   barrier inequality, invariant law, reversibility assumption, and exact
   theorem hypothesis that discharges each row. The packet's assertion at
   lines 374-378 is not a substitute for these matches.

5. **Reshaping checks.** For `h=id+g`, prove the regularity and strict
   monotonicity needed to preserve the domain, critical set, flow direction,
   and boundary; show `h` and `g` are fixed as `beta -> infinity`; and derive
   the transformed cost `h(cap)-h(J(q))`. The class description at lines
   152-154 and the display at 253-259 do not themselves discharge these
   analytic hypotheses.

6. **Coupling checks.** For `E_epsilon` and `E_c`, supply the exact coupled
   exit domain, ground/pinning result, boundary barrier, treatment of neutral
   rotation modes, and the dependence of constants and thresholds on
   `epsilon`, budgets, `N`, `kappa`, and `lambda`. State whether `epsilon` is
   fixed before `beta -> infinity`; a pointwise small-coupling result does
   not automatically give a uniform joint limit.

7. **Domain versus label event.** For a domain certificate, show precisely
   how B.2 turns `tau_domain` into a lower bound on label constancy. For a
   label certificate, B.1-B.2 are insufficient: serve the ridge/crossing
   proof that every sector-changing path pays the one-bond saddle level
   `S_k`, plus the corresponding action application. The packet itself says
   `S_k > 2 kappa` (lines 207-247).

8. **Two certified forms.** Derive both the high-probability survival form
   and the expectation liminf with their exact quantifiers. If expectation
   is obtained from the probability statement, write that implication and
   its uniformity explicitly rather than citing both as independent outputs.

9. **Non-reversible boundary.** The packet mentions skew/state-dependent
   persistence at lines 89-91 but calls the composed stack reversible-only
   at 94-96 and sharpness reversible-only at 193-204. Narrow the broad
   "every persistence display" sentence at lines 374-378, or serve a
   separate action theorem for every non-reversible use. A reversible
   gradient theorem cannot be silently transported to a skew drift.

These demands are substantive. Under the manifest's decision, Q7 should
therefore open the scoped follow-up serving the five P0 persistence
artifacts; nothing in this verdict pre-judges that follow-up.

### 5. Whatever breaks — the packet contains a stale, directly contradictory Q9 description

Packet lines 237-245 insert an explicit correction: the product saddle is
`E_epsilon`-critical at every `epsilon`, is located and analyzed, never
moves, and is **not** "bypassed, never analyzed." But the reader question at
lines 459-464 still describes the product tube trick as one in which "the
perturbed saddle is bypassed." A reader cannot know whether "bypassed" now
means only that one proof route avoids a local calculation or repeats the
withdrawn claim. Replace Q9(b) with the corrected object-level statement
before human dispatch.

### 6. Whatever breaks — the assurance taxonomy drifts from the permitted grade block

The packet defines `L-A2f` as a blind audit by a different model with no repo
access and omits `L-A2` (packet lines 469-476). The canonical grade block
defines `L-A2f` by **same-provider-family** model diversity and `L-A2` by a
different provider architecture (`canon/CANON-LADDER.md`, lines 369-377).
Blindness/access mode may be an additional protocol qualifier, but it is not
the canonical distinction between these rungs. As written, a reader can
misread the claimed decorrelation strength. Use the canonical rung
definitions verbatim, then state blindness and audit multiplicity as
separate qualifiers.

### 7. Whatever breaks — the coupled lower-bound display is not self-contained

The display at packet lines 233-235 contains
`S_k - m_k - epsilon w_k - C epsilon^2`, but `w_k` is never defined in the
packet. Because `w` already denotes winding, the symbol is especially
ambiguous. Define `w_k` (and the dependence/sign convention for `C`) at its
first use or remove the display from a packet advertised as self-contained.

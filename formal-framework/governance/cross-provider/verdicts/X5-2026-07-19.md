### S1 — what checks, and what does not

**Detailed balance checks directly.** For

`L f = beta^-1 Delta f - <grad E, grad f>` and `rho = Z^-1 exp(-beta E)`,

one has

`L f = beta^-1 exp(beta E) div(exp(-beta E) grad f)`.

Integration by parts on the compact torus makes `L` symmetric in `L^2(rho)`. Smooth uniform ellipticity supplies a continuous positive heat kernel, so self-adjointness gives `rho(x)p_t(x,y)=rho(y)p_t(y,x)` first almost everywhere and then pointwise by continuity. Iterating this identity through a finite-dimensional path density gives the reversal identity used in SH.1. I found no algebraic error in that telescoping step.

**The non-gradient action inequality checks.** With `ell=A grad J`, `A^T=-A`, and `b=-grad J+ell`,

`|dot(phi)-b|^2 = |dot(phi)-ell-grad J|^2 + 4 d/dt J(phi)`.

Thus `I_T >= J(phi(T))-J(phi(0))`. The same calculation is pointwise for a smooth state-dependent antisymmetric `A(theta)` and the limit drift `b_inf`; no derivative of `A` enters this algebraic identity. S2.2 and M1b.2 are sound.

### S4 — explicit refutation of SH.2'(b)

`E(r,s)=1-cos r`, and `G={r=0} x T`.

`ds_t = sqrt(2/beta) dB_t` on the circle.

Start at `(0,0)`. Let `S_eta` be a small positive-volume rectangle centered near `(0,pi)`, with radial width below `delta`, so `S_eta subset B_delta(G)`. The time-one circle heat kernel at antipodal displacement has exponential scale

`p_1(0,pi) = sqrt(beta/(4pi)) sum_{n in Z} exp(-beta (pi+2pi n)^2/4)`

and therefore costs `exp(-beta pi^2/4)` up to polynomial factors (or `exp(-beta (pi-eta)^2/4)` for a fixed-width target). The radial corridor condition cannot increase this probability.

### S6 — Morse-Bott band and the copy issue

For a compact Morse-Bott critical orbit `Z`, tubular coordinates give, uniformly in `z in Z`,

`E_0(z+v)-S_k = (1/2)<H_z v,v> + O(|v|^3)`

and

`grad E_0(z+v)=H_z v+O(|v|^2)`.

Compactness plus transverse nondegeneracy gives a uniform upper Hessian norm and a positive minimum transverse singular value. After shrinking the tube, these yield

`|E_0-S_k| <= C_Z dist(.,Z)^2` and `|grad E_0| >= c_Z dist(.,Z)`.

Thus LP.1(i)–(iii) are mathematically standard and sound for fixed `N` under the stated Morse-Bott input. The constants are not uniform in `N`, and the artifact does not claim they are.

The union qualifier is load-bearing. There are `N` cyclic hot-bond copies of the saddle orbit. If `T_r(Z)` removes only one copy, another copy has `E_0=S_k` and `grad E_0=0` while remaining in `K_all`, so `c_shell=0`. The present file explicitly records this counterexample and orders every `Z` tube/excision to be read as the union of all `N` copies. Under that reading, the copies are a finite compact Morse-Bott union and the argument survives.

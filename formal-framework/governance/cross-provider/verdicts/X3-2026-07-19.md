## FINDINGS

### 1. MAJOR — the product saddle is exactly critical, contrary to K10's standing premise

The declared energy is

`E_eps(d,e)=J_kappa(d)+J_lambda(e)+eps sum_i(1-cos(d_i-e_i))`.

At `d=spBond=(pi-a,a,...,a)` and `e=0`, all `sin d_i` equal `sin a`. Therefore the two criticality chains recorded in M2P.1 are constant:

```text
kappa sin d_i + eps sin(d_i-e_i) = (kappa+eps) sin a,
lambda sin e_i - eps sin(d_i-e_i) = -eps sin a.
```

Every realizable bond variation has zero sum, so both differentials vanish. Thus `Z=SP_k x C_0` is `E_eps`-critical for every `eps`, not merely at `eps=0`. Its exact level is

```text
E_eps(Z) = S_k + eps S_k/kappa.
```

There is also a stronger exact Morse-Bott statement. Put `c=cos a>0`, `alpha=(N-2)/N`, and decompose the zero-sum bond space into the `(N-2)`-dimensional stable subspace and the one-dimensional hot-bond mode. On each stable mode the paired quadratic form is

```text
c kappa x^2 + lambda y^2 + c eps (x-y)^2 > 0.
```

On the hot-bond mode it is

```text
-alpha c kappa x^2 + lambda y^2 - alpha c eps (x-y)^2,
```

whose determinant is

```text
-alpha c [kappa lambda + eps (lambda-alpha c kappa)] < 0
```

under `lambda>kappa`. Hence the full angle-space inertia is `(2N-3 positive, 1 negative, 2 rotation-zero)` for every `eps>=0` under M2-PIN. This is a useful missing lemma and removes the need to speak of unknown nearby perturbed saddles.

Impact: LP.2's tube exclusion still works because it uses `E_eps>=E_0`, not noncriticality. LP.4 already permits all `E_eps` criticals to lie in its inner core. LP.5 already charges the exact linear level contribution `eps S_k/kappa`. The false premise should nevertheless be repaired in `lane-lp`, `lane-lt`, the reader packet, and canon, and the stronger exact saddle lemma should replace the “not located” narrative.

### 2. LOAD-BEARING REFUTATION — SH.2'(b) is false and the along-G chain does not compose

The counterexample is a valid abstract working family. Take `X=T^2`, coordinates `(r,theta)`, `E(r,theta)=1-cos r`, `cap=1`, and `D=(-pi/2,pi/2) x T`, the connected sub-cap component around `G={r=0}`. Then (F1) holds, `Crit(E) intersect closure(D)=G` and every descent converges to `G` (F2), and both boundary circles are regular level-`1` sets (F3). The gradient drift has no tangential component, so from `(0,0)` the time-one probability of a small target near `(0,pi)` has tangential large-deviation cost `pi^2/4`, while SH.2'(b) assigns every target inside the global tube `B_delta(G)` the vanishing cost `C_G=O(delta^2)`. For sufficiently small `delta`, the claimed lower bound exceeds the true probability exponentially as `beta -> infinity`.

There are also four independent proof failures:

- the written corridor drift estimate drops a multiplicative corridor radius;
- a Gaussian endpoint density for the driving increment is not automatically a density lower bound for the nonlinear SDE endpoint;
- compact connectedness alone supplies finite epsilon-chains, not the linear chain-complexity bound needed for `n(delta)delta^2 -> 0`;
- even on a smooth orbit, the displayed iteration uses a hop scale `h=delta/(2C*)` but lands at radius `C*h`, so the next invocation's radius-`h` start hypothesis is not recovered.

For the actual corpus instances, the ground sets are smooth compact group orbits, so a **local** killed-kernel/Girsanov estimate with a target ball near the next orbit point, corrected Gronwall constants, and an explicit finite intrinsic-diameter/quasiconvexity lemma appears capable of repairing the route. Until that replacement is stated and proved, K6 and every upper/sharp law that consumes it do not have the advertised support.
